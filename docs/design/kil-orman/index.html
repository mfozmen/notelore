Notelore: Kil (day) and Orman (night)

The chosen design: one theme, two modes. Day is Kil (cream and terracotta), night is Orman (green-black and clay orange). Layout, fonts and shapes are the same in both modes; only the colours change. A hand-drawn mockup, not app code.

Headings in Fraunces (serif), body in Plus Jakarta Sans, labels in JetBrains Mono capitals. Cards are rounded with a thin border. The assistant answers without a bubble; a decision it finds shows as a coloured "hot card". The mic is a glowing orb. The Notes screen opens with the open todos that are due soon ("Coming up"); long titles clamp to two lines.
